We study tyre rubber blends. Using the polyBERT model (https://huggingface.co/xushijie/polyBERT), compute the fingerprint of a blend of 70 % natural rubber ([*]CC(C)=CC[*]) and 30 % polybutadiene ([*]CC=CC[*]) and of a blend of 70 % styrene-butadiene rubber ([*]CC=CCCC([*])c1ccccc1) and 30 % polybutadiene. Report the cosine similarity between the two blend fingerprints and the first five numbers of each.
